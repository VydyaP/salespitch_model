## Facts

- Snowman Logistics was incorporated in 1993 and is headquartered in Bengaluru, operating as the leading temperature-controlled logistics provider on a pan-India basis. ([source](https://www.bitget.com/stock/nse-snowman/what-is))
- The company operates 50 warehouses (cold storage and dry) across 3 million+ sq ft, with a total capacity of approximately 143,000+ pallets across 40+ strategic locations as of Q3 FY2025. ([source](https://www.bitget.com/stock/nse-snowman/what-is))
- Snowman operates a fleet of 600+ trucks, including primary and secondary refrigerated (reefer) vehicles, to keep the cold chain unbroken during transport. ([source](https://www.bitget.com/stock/nse-snowman/what-is))
- Snowman serves pharmaceuticals, quick-service restaurants (QSR), seafood, and e-commerce clients, and offers value-added services like blast freezing, kitting, labeling, sorting, and repacking. ([source](https://supplychaindigital.com/logistics/snowman-logistics-delivering-leading-cold-chain-logistics))
- In July 2026, Snowman announced a 10,000-pallet expansion of its Hyderabad facility (to 16,800 pallets total) under an asset-light Built-to-Suit model, with temperature control from -25°C to +20°C. ([source](https://www.sahi.com/news/snowman-logistics-expands-hyderabad-capacity-by-10-000-pallets-to-reach-16-800-under-built-to-suit-expansion-4105-PE1_COR))
- Beyond Hyderabad, Snowman is expanding in Pune (adding a 5,900-pallet facility by June 2026 on top of an existing 16,000-pallet site) and has projects underway in Kolkata, Krishnapatnam, and Patna. ([source](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/snowman-logistics-plans-new-6-500-pallet-temperature-controlled-warehouse-in-patna-by-january-2027/34933098))
- Gateway Distriparks is Snowman Logistics' parent/promoter company. Adani Logistics (a subsidiary of Adani Ports & SEZ) attempted to acquire a 40.25% stake in 2019 for INR 296 crore; after a 2020 dispute settlement it retained a minority ~26% stake while Gateway Distriparks remained sole promoter and manager. ([source](https://www.ibef.org/news/adani-ports-acquires-4025-per-cent-stake-in-snowman-logistics))

## Gaps (3 found — 2 high, 1 medium)

### 🔴 [HIGH] Rapid multi-site rollout with no stated unified monitoring platform

Snowman is expanding or opening at least five facilities in 2026 alone (Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Krishnapatnam, Patna) under an asset-light Built-to-Suit model. Fast, geographically dispersed rollout like this raises the risk of siloed, per-site temperature monitoring and inconsistent compliance reporting unless every new warehouse is tied into one platform from day one.

**GND service:** Warehouse Management + Platform (ThinxFresh)

> Based on: Beyond Hyderabad, Snowman is expanding in Pune (adding a 5,900-pallet facility by June 2026 on top of an existing 16,000-pallet site) and has projects underway in Kolkata, Krishnapatnam, and Patna; also the July 2026 Hyderabad Built-to-Suit expansion to 16,800 pallets.

### 🔴 [HIGH] Pharma cold-chain compliance load without confirmed automated audit reporting

Snowman explicitly serves pharmaceutical clients across facilities running -25°C to +20°C. India's Revised Schedule M and WHO-GDP/ALCOA+ rules require continuous deviation monitoring and audit-ready records at that scale; without a named automated compliance layer, per-site manual logging is a real regulatory exposure as pharma volume grows.

**GND service:** Cold Chain Management (ThinxFresh)

> Based on: Snowman serves pharmaceuticals, quick-service restaurants (QSR), seafood, and e-commerce clients; the Hyderabad facility offers temperature-controlled storage from -25°C to +20°C.

### 🟠 [MEDIUM] 600+ vehicle reefer fleet without a stated real-time asset-tracking layer

A 600+ truck fleet moving perishable and pharma cargo pan-India is a large, distributed, high-value asset base. In-transit cold-chain breaches are typically harder to catch than in-warehouse ones, and no GPS/telematics or tamper-detection platform is named in available research.

**GND service:** Asset Tracking & Monitoring

> Based on: Snowman operates a fleet of 600+ trucks, including primary and secondary refrigerated (reefer) vehicles.
