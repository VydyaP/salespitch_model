## Facts

- Blue Dart Express was founded in November 1983 by Tushar Jani, Khushroo Dubash, and Clyde Cooper, and is headquartered in Mumbai. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Dart_Express))
- DHL Express (Singapore) Pte Ltd holds a 75% majority stake in Blue Dart Express (since November 2012), after originally acquiring 81.03% following a 2002 sales alliance and later investment. ([source](https://www.bluedart.com/blue-dart-dhl-relationship))
- Blue Dart reported ₹5,720 crore revenue for FY25 (15% growth), and handles roughly 8% of India's e-commerce shipments as of 2024. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Dart_Express))
- In January 2025, Blue Dart opened India's largest low-emission integrated logistics center (2.5 lakh sq ft) at Bijwasan, Delhi, capable of handling 550,000 inbound and outbound shipments daily. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Dart_Express))
- Blue Dart offers Temperature Controlled Logistics as a specialized service across three ranges -- Frozen (-20°C), Chilled (2-8°C), Ambient (15-25°C) -- for pharmaceuticals, clinical research organizations, and hospitals, covering items like blood, plasma, tissues, life-saving drugs, and diagnostics kits across 53 origin-destination pairs. ([source](https://www.bluedart.com/temperature-controlled-logistics))
- For temperature-controlled shipments, Blue Dart provides real-time online location tracking, but temperature logger graphs are only delivered to the shipper 24-48 hours after the shipment has already been delivered. ([source](https://www.bluedart.com/temperature-controlled-logistics))
