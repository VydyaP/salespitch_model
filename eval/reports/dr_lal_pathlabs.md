## Facts

- Dr Lal PathLabs was founded in 1949 by Dr. (Major) S.K. Lal, who laid the foundation of what became the first respected pathology lab in Delhi and Northern India. ([source](https://www.lalpathlabs.com/blog/a-quick-glance-the-foundation-of-dr-lal-pathlabs/))
- The company now operates 310+ satellite labs, 7,725+ patient service centres, and 13,930+ pick-up points, has collected 94.5M+ samples across 30.3M+ customers, and operates in 28+ countries. ([source](https://www.lalpathlabs.com/))
- The company is accredited by NABL, CAP, and ISO9001:2008, operates a National Reference Laboratory at Rohini and a Kolkata Reference Laboratory, and describes its quality process only generically as '50+ Quality Checks' -- no specific temperature-monitoring technology is named. ([source](https://www.lalpathlabs.com/))
- Dr Lal PathLabs operates a hub-and-spoke model -- a reference lab, satellite labs, collection centers, a logistics team, and a home collection team -- with samples barcoded with a unique ID so the company can track every step until the report is uploaded. ([source](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-rctom/submission/dr-lal-pathlabs-achieving-scale-in-indian-diagnostics-market/))

### 🔴 [HIGH] Massive sample network, only generic 'quality checks' named -- no temperature monitoring
