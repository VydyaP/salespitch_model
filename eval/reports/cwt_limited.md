## Facts

- CWT was founded in 1970 as a private arm of the Port of Singapore Authority; in 2017 HNA Group completed a roughly S$1.4 billion (US$1 billion) acquisition and became controlling shareholder, renaming the company CWT International Limited. ([source](https://www.acnnewswire.com/press-release/english/40407/hna-holding-completes-acquisition-of-singapore-company-cwt-limited-and-changes-name-to-cwt-international-limited))
- CWT operates a 750,000-sq. ft. multi-temperature-controlled cold hub in Singapore with -18°C freezer, air-conditioned, and ambient storage, serving food manufacturers, wholesalers, and retailers. ([source](https://www.cwtlimited.com/cold-chain-logistics/))
- The facility uses a front-end iSCM platform linked to customer ERP systems, a WMS for cargo movements, and a temperature monitoring and tracking system, though no specifics on deviation-alert speed or automated compliance reporting are given. ([source](https://www.cwtlimited.com/cold-chain-logistics/))
- The facility holds six separate certifications -- ISO9001, ISO14001, ISO22000, ISO22339, ISO45000, and HACCP -- and maintains separate storage for Halal and non-Halal products alongside an on-site AVA (food safety authority) Inspection Room. ([source](https://www.cwtlimited.com/cold-chain-logistics/))
