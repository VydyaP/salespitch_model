## Facts

- Zomato Ltd's shareholders approved renaming the company to Eternal Limited (announced Feb 6, 2025, effective March 20, 2025); Hyperpure operates as the B2B venture within the Eternal group alongside Blinkit (quick commerce) and District (events), while the Zomato brand continues for food delivery. ([source](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/06/indias-zomato-to-rebrand-as-eternal))
- Eternal Limited's registered office is in New Delhi with a corporate office in Gurugram; the company was incorporated in 2010 and formally renamed from Zomato Limited on March 20, 2025. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternal_Limited))
- In under a year, Zomato Hyperpure leased more than 5 lakh sq ft of warehousing across two sites in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region: 2.5 lakh sq ft in Bhiwandi (5-year lease signed August 2025, ~Rs 66.21 lakh/month rent from March 2026) and 2.53 lakh sq ft in Palava (5-year lease from February 15, 2025, ~Rs 85.3 lakh/month), both with 5% annual rent escalation. ([source](https://leasewarehouse.in/blog/post_details/246))
- The Bhiwandi warehouse will support Hyperpure's sourcing and distribution of fresh and packaged products -- fruits, vegetables, meat, seafood, groceries, and essentials -- for hotels, restaurants, caterers, and food businesses. ([source](https://www.logisticsinsider.in/zomato-hyperpure-expands-b2b-supply-chain-with-major-warehouse-lease-in-bhiwandi/))
