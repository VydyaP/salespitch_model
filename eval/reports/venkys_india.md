## Facts

- The VH Group was founded in 1971 by Dr. B.V. Rao, who established Venkateshwara Hatcheries Pvt. Ltd. in Pune; the group is now the largest fully integrated poultry group in Asia. ([source](https://venkys.com/about-us/))
- Venky's (India) Limited operates through three segments -- Poultry and Poultry Products (day-old chicks, SPF eggs, processed chicken, poultry feed), Animal Health Products, and Oilseed. ([source](https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202601140118_Venky%60s_(india)_Limited.pdf))
- In FY25, Venky's reported total operating income of Rs 3,308.19 crore (down from Rs 3,738.66 crore in FY24), with PBILDT improving to Rs 173.05 crore (FY24: Rs 124.89 crore) at a 5.23% margin. ([source](https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202601140118_Venky%60s_(india)_Limited.pdf))
- Venkateshwara Hatcheries' poultry operations are based across Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and the southern states of India, and the company sells processed chicken through its own retail brand, Venky's Xprs. ([source](https://www.indiamart.com/venkateshwarahatcheries/))

## Pitch

**Angle:** Venky's is Asia's largest fully integrated poultry group, moving processed chicken from production facilities across Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and southern India into its own Xprs retail network -- but nothing in its public materials names a monitoring system tracking that journey. For a fresh/frozen protein business operating at this scale and multi-state spread, GND's ThinxFresh would give one consistent, auditable view from processing plant to retail counter instead of assuming everything held cold in between.

**Opener:** With processed chicken moving from your Maharashtra, MP, and southern India facilities into your own Xprs outlets, what's tracking temperature consistency across that whole journey today?
