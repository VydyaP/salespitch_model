## Facts

- Vadilal made its first ice cream in 1907 in Ahmedabad using the traditional kothi method with a hand-cranked machine, founded by Vadilal Gandhi; the company went public via a 1989 IPO and trades on both BSE and NSE. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vadilal))
- Vadilal operates a Cold Chain Network comprising three manufacturing plants (Pundhra and Dharampur in Gujarat, and Bareilly in Uttar Pradesh) totaling a production capacity of 1.25 lakh litres per day, about 23 C&FA, more than 500 distributors, and over 40,000 retailers. ([source](https://www.indiamart.com/vadilal-industries-ltd-ahmedabad/aboutus.html))
- Vadilal offers 120+ flavors across more than 250 pack forms, and holds roughly a quarter of India's Rs. 15 billion (US$330 million) ice cream industry. ([source](https://www.indiamart.com/vadilal-industries-ltd-ahmedabad/aboutus.html))
- The Pundhra plant holds Export Inspection Council of India certification for exports, and Vadilal is recognized as one of the pioneers of India's frozen food export market, including processed vegetables, fruit, ready meals, snacks, ice cream and desserts. ([source](https://www.indiamart.com/vadilal-industries-ltd-ahmedabad/aboutus.html))
