---

## Facts

- RSA Cold Chain is based in Dubai and began operations in 2017 as a subsidiary of RSA Global, holding HACCP and ISO 22000 certifications, serving packaged food, fresh produce, dairy, and frozen foods. ([source](https://ir.americold.com/press-releases/news-details/2023/Americold-Announces-Strategic-Investment-in-Dubai-Cold-Storage-Company-RSA-Cold-Chain/default.aspx))
- The Dubai facility spans 46,000 sqm with 62,000 pallet positions, offering a flexible -25°C to +25°C range per chamber, with frozen storage at -25°C to -18°C and chilled storage at 0°C to 5°C. ([source](https://www.rsa.global/cold-storage))
- The facility holds ISO 9001, ISO 22000, ISO 14001, and OHSAS 18001 certifications. ([source](https://www.rsa.global/cold-storage))
- RSA's own marketing claims an advanced WMS for real-time inventory visibility, continuous temperature monitoring, and a single-integration API for real-time inventory data. ([source](https://www.rsa.global/cold-storage))
- RSA Cold Chain serves Retail & Grocery, Wholesale & Distributors, Quick Commerce, QSR, HORECA, and food producers -- pharmaceuticals is not listed among its served industries. ([source](https://www.rsa.global/cold-storage))
- In 2023, Americold acquired a 49% minority stake in RSA Cold Chain for $3.9 million, with immediate plans to expand locally, connect RSA to its global network, and pursue development opportunities across the Middle East and India, leveraging a concurrent partnership with DP World. ([source](https://ir.americold.com/press-releases/news-details/2023/Americold-Announces-Strategic-Investment-in-Dubai-Cold-Storage-Company-RSA-Cold-Chain/default.aspx))
