## Facts

- Interstate Cold Storage was founded in 1973 by Vincent Tippmann in Fort Wayne, Indiana, and grew over 50 years from a one-warehouse operation into the Midwest's premier cold storage provider, ranked a Top 20 North American refrigerated warehousing company by the International Association of Refrigerated Warehouses (IARW). ([source](https://interstatecoldstorage.com/ics-50th-year-2023/))
- The family-owned company operates five locations across Indiana and Ohio (including two in Fort Wayne and one in Columbus) totaling nearly 22 million cubic feet of refrigerated space, with multi-room, multi-temp facilities supporting temperature ranges from -15°F to 35°F. ([source](https://interstatecoldstorage.com/locations/))
- The Fort Wayne West facility offers storage from -13°F to 0°F, can process up to 250,000 pounds per day with quick-freezing capability, and is equipped with EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) and customer internet access for real-time inventory tracking, integrated with ammonia refrigeration systems. ([source](https://interstatecoldstorage.com/fort-wayne-west-interstate-cold-storage-2/))
