## Facts

- Coldrush Logistics was established in 2012 and is headquartered in Lower Parel, Mumbai. ([source](https://coldrushlogistics.com/about.php))
- The company operates 202 vehicles (Ashok Leyland and Tata Motors chassis), including 20 newly developed reefer trucks specifically for safe handling of pharmaceutical products, especially vaccines. ([source](https://www.motorindiaonline.in/coldrush-logistics-primed-for-rapid-growth/))
- Coldrush maintains 12 warehouses nationwide with a total pallet-holding capacity of 32,000. ([source](https://www.motorindiaonline.in/coldrush-logistics-primed-for-rapid-growth/))
- The company serves over 300 customers across pharmaceuticals, FMCG, QSR, meat/seafood, and dairy, with roughly 30-40% of its business derived from the pharmaceutical industry. ([source](https://www.motorindiaonline.in/coldrush-logistics-primed-for-rapid-growth/))
- Coldrush plans to add another 150-200 trucks to reach 100,000 pallets of capacity within five years, and to open cold storages and branch offices in the capital city of each north-eastern 'seven sisters' state within 3-5 years. ([source](https://www.motorindiaonline.in/coldrush-logistics-primed-for-rapid-growth/))
- The company employs 400+ drivers, key account managers, and other professionals with an average of over 20 years of experience in logistics and warehousing. ([source](https://www.motorindiaonline.in/coldrush-logistics-primed-for-rapid-growth/))
- Coldrush's own marketing describes 'latest cold chain tech for seamless, transparent operations with real-time data access' but does not name a specific monitoring platform, vendor, or IoT system. ([source](https://coldrushlogistics.com/about.php))
