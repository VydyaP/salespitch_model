## Facts

- Preferred Freezer Services was founded in 1989. ([source](https://jobs.assp.org/profile/preferred-freezer-services-llc/839686))
- The company’s headquarters are located at One Main St, Chatham, New Jersey 07928, United States. ([source](https://rocketreach.co/preferred-freezer-services-profile_b5c1b688f42e08d4))
- Preferred Freezer Services designs, constructs, and operates state‑of‑the‑art temperature‑controlled warehouses in the United States and internationally (including China and Vietnam). ([source](https://www.seafoodsource.com/news/business-finance/preferred-freezer-acquisition-a-significant-milestone-for-the-temperature-controlled-logistics-industry))
- The company employs approximately 1,300 people. ([source](https://jobs.assp.org/profile/preferred-freezer-services-llc/839686))
- John J. Galiher serves as Chief Executive Officer. ([source](https://jobs.assp.org/profile/preferred-freezer-services-llc/839686))
- Preferred Freezer Services was acquired by Lineage Logistics in February 2019. ([source](https://www.onelineage.com/news-stories/lineage-logistics-announces-acquisition-preferred-freezer-services))
- After the acquisition, the combined entity has over 1.3 billion cubic feet of temperature‑controlled capacity across more than 200 facilities worldwide. ([source](https://www.refrigeratedfrozenfood.com/articles/96621-lineage-logistics-acquires-preferred-freezer-services))
- The acquisition was backed by Bay Grove, the principal investment firm supporting Lineage Logistics. ([source](https://www.onelineage.com/news-stories/lineage-logistics-announces-acquisition-preferred-freezer-services))
- Preferred Freezer Services is now a private subsidiary of Lineage Logistics Holdings, LLC. ([source](https://www.owler.com/company/preferredfreezer))
- Post‑merger, the combined company operates 17 automated facilities with more than 800,000 automated pallet positions. ([source](https://www.roi-nj.com/2019/02/25/industry/lineage-logistics-acquires-n-j-based-preferred-freezer-solutions))
