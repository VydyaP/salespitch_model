## Facts

- Hatsun Agro Product was established in 1970 and is the largest private-sector dairy in India, headquartered in Chennai. ([source](https://www.ibef.org/industry/indian-food-industry/showcase/hatsun-agro))
- Over 4 lakh (400,000+) farmers deliver milk daily across more than 10,000 Hatsun Milk Banks (HMBs) covering over 10,000 villages, via more than 1,100 rural milk procurement routes. ([source](https://www.hap.in/milk-procurement.php))
- Hatsun deploys Active Bulk Coolers (ABC) at more than 1,100 village-level locations for instant chilling of milk within 2 hours of collection. ([source](https://www.hap.in/milk-procurement.php))
- The farmers' database is managed through a cloud-based software system with data transferred daily via EKO machines; milk is tested for fat and SNF at collection points, with additional testing at Milk Chilling Centers before tanker transport to facilities. ([source](https://www.hap.in/milk-procurement.php))
- Hatsun operates chilling centres in more than 68 locations with over 1,348 contract vehicles, milk sheds across 10 districts in Tamil Nadu and 3 districts in Karnataka, and 20 manufacturing plants across 7 states as of 2025. ([source](https://www.ibef.org/industry/indian-food-industry/showcase/hatsun-agro))
