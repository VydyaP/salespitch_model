---

## Facts

- Karras Cold Logistics was founded in 1976 by Eddie Karras, is 100% Australian and family-owned, and grew for over 40 years purely through word of mouth and referrals rather than aggressive expansion. ([source](https://karras.com.au/about-us/))
- The company processes thousands of weekly consignments spanning dairy, seafood, poultry, and confectionery for manufacturers, wholesalers, importers, and retailers. ([source](https://karras.com.au/about-us/))
- Karras has a combined storage capacity of over 25,000 pallets across Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane warehouses (plus an Adelaide location), offering chilled storage (0 to 4 degrees) and frozen storage (-18 to -24 degrees). ([source](https://karras.com.au/temperature-controlled-warehousing/))
- Karras describes its technology only as scanning products accurately into the warehouse with system integrations offered, with no specifics on real-time monitoring or deviation alerting. ([source](https://karras.com.au/temperature-controlled-warehousing/))
- In November 2023, NewCold -- a Netherlands-based global leader in advanced food logistics founded in 2012 that specializes in automated temperature-controlled storage -- acquired Karras Group via a competitive bid process, explicitly to expand its Australian footprint and operating model. ([source](https://hallandwilcox.com.au/news/hall-wilcox-advises-newcold-on-acquisition-of-karras-group/))
