## Facts

- Burris Logistics is a family‑owned third‑party logistics company delivering dependable supply chain solutions. ([source](https://www.linkedin.com/company/burrislogistics))
- Through its three companies—Honor Foods, Trinity Logistics, and Eagle Business Credit—Burris provides foodservice redistribution, freight management, and innovative technology, operating under the ONEBURRIS brand. ([source](https://www.linkedin.com/company/burrislogistics))
- The company specializes in custom retail distribution, foodservice redistribution, public refrigerated warehousing, and transportation solutions. ([source](https://www.supplychain247.com/company/burris_logistics))
- Burris Logistics offers over 60 million cubic feet of freezer warehousing space across 13 strategic locations on the East Coast. ([source](https://www.supplychain247.com/company/burris_logistics))
- The company reported revenue of $805.7 million. ([source](https://rocketreach.co/burris-logistics-profile_b5c6ca2ff42e0cc9))
- Employee count is listed as 501‑1,000 on LinkedIn and 1,000‑5,000 on ZoomInfo. ([source](https://www.linkedin.com/company/burrislogistics))
- On October 3 2023, Burris divested its PRW‑specific and e‑commerce business to Lineage Logistics. ([source](https://www.burrislogistics.com/why-burris/history))
- Burris Logistics acquired Eagle Business Credit and its subsidiary Cashway Funding. ([source](https://www.burrislogistics.com/why-burris/history))
- The company operates temperature‑controlled facilities in Sumter, South Carolina; Benson, North Carolina; and Chesapeake, Virginia. ([source](https://www.burrislogistics.com/why-burris/history))
- Key specialties include logistics, freight brokerage, logistics technology, foodservice redistribution, supply chain, purchasing team, and a dedicated fleet. ([source](https://www.linkedin.com/company/burrislogistics))
