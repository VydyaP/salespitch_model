## Facts

- The Radhakrishna Group was founded in 1966, with the company's modern cold chain, QSR service, and food retail operations beginning in 1986 -- giving it 38+ years of cold chain industry experience. ([source](https://rkfoodland.com/))
- The company operates 2 million+ square feet of warehousing space across 30+ distribution centers in India, reaching over 300 cities and 5,000+ drop points/restaurants, staffed by 3,000+ trained employees. ([source](https://rkfoodland.com/))
- Radhakrishna Foodland runs 600+ multi-temperature transport assets and reports a 99% on-time delivery performance rate. ([source](https://rkfoodland.com/))
- Warehousing covers frozen, chilled, tropical, and ambient storage, and the operation is BRCGS certified and FSSAI compliant for food safety. ([source](https://rkfoodland.com/solutions/cold-chain-warehousing))
- The company describes using 'advanced inventory management technologies' for real-time visibility and control, claiming 99.8%+ inventory accuracy, though it does not name a specific IoT or monitoring platform. ([source](https://rkfoodland.com/solutions/cold-chain-warehousing))
- In 2024 the company introduced its own 'Foodland Operating System' and a supply-chain 'control tower' for data-driven visibility, having expanded operations into the Middle East in 2021. ([source](https://rkfoodland.com/))
