## Facts

- TCI Cold Chain Solutions is a joint venture between Transport Corporation of India (TCI, 80%) and Japan's Mitsui & Co. Ltd. (20%); Mitsui invested in November 2021, citing expected rapid growth in India's cold chain sector driven by pharma demand. ([source](https://www.itln.in/mitsui-invests-in-tcis-cold-chain-logistics-business))
- At the time of Mitsui's November 2021 investment, TCI CCS operated 130+ refrigerated vehicles and multi-temperature warehouses totaling 12 million sq. ft. of storage capacity, serving pharmaceuticals, fresh food, chemicals, and quick-service restaurants. ([source](https://www.itln.in/mitsui-invests-in-tcis-cold-chain-logistics-business))
- TCI Cold Chain Solutions now manages 17 million sq. ft. of storage space with 22,000+ pallet positions across a -18°C to +25°C temperature range and 35%-65% relative humidity control, with a workforce of 3,500+. ([source](https://tciccs.com/about-us/))
- TCI follows compliance with FDA, FSSAI, NBC, and CMVR standards with internal audits, and serves fruits & veggies, dairy, meat, aqua, frozen food, confectionery, floriculture, pharmaceuticals, and health sciences verticals. ([source](https://tciccs.com/about-us/))
- In December 2025, TCI opened a new 1.5-lakh-sq.-ft. cold chain facility at Taj Nagar, Gurugram, featuring SCADA-based monitoring, VESDA early smoke detection, 600+ CCTV cameras, and 500 kW rooftop solar, with multi-temperature (frozen/chilled/ambient) zones. Nationwide managed warehousing now exceeds 16 million sq. ft. ([source](https://indiashippingnews.com/tci-adds-1-5-lakh-sq-ft-cold-chain-capacity-at-taj-nagar-gurugram/))

## Pitch

**Angle:** TCI has clearly invested in serious facility-level infrastructure — SCADA, VESDA, hundreds of CCTV cameras — as it's grown from 12M to 17M+ sq ft since the Mitsui JV in 2021. But that scale and pace of expansion is exactly where facility-by-facility systems start to show cracks: an auditor asking for one deviation report across the whole -18°C to +25°C network is a very different request than pulling logs from 20+ separately-built sites. GND's ThinxFresh doesn't replace what TCI already has — it sits on top, turning per-site SCADA/CCTV investment into one real-time, audit-ready view across FDA, FSSAI, NBC, and CMVR compliance at once.
