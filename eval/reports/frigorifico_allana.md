## Facts

- The Allana Group was founded in 1865 by Abdulla Allana and is now the largest private food company in India, processing and selling over one million metric tons of products annually to 85+ countries. ([source](https://allana.com/our-heritage/))
- Allanasons Private Limited, the group's export entity, is headquartered at Allana Centre on MG Road, Mumbai, and reports annual turnover of over 500 crore INR. ([source](https://www.allanagroup.in/))
- Frigorifico Allana's frozen meat operations have an annual production capacity of 750,000 MTS and cold storage capacity of 66,000 MTS spread across ten facilities, exporting to over 70 countries. ([source](https://allana.com/proteins/frozen-meat/))
- Operations are ISO 9001, FSSC 22000, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 and HACCP process-compliant, following Codex Alimentarius standards and OIE guidelines, with meat exports prepared to Halal requirements. ([source](https://allana.com/proteins/frozen-meat/))
