---

## Facts

- On May 31, 2025, the Maharashtra FDA inspected Zepto's (KiranKart Technologies Pvt. Ltd.) Dharavi warehouse in Mumbai and found fungal growth, improper storage, and inadequate cold storage -- dairy products not held at the mandated 0-4°C and frozen items failing the -18°C requirement -- plus expired products stored alongside fresh stock, resulting in license suspension under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006. ([source](https://www.logisticsinsider.in/zepto-fda-raid-exposes-critical-vulnerabilities-in-quick-commerce-cold-chain/))
- Following the Zepto suspension, Maharashtra's Minister of State for FDA, Yogesh Kadam, called for inspections of all quick-commerce facilities in the state, including competitors Blinkit and Instamart. ([source](https://www.logisticsinsider.in/zepto-fda-raid-exposes-critical-vulnerabilities-in-quick-commerce-cold-chain/))
- Zepto was founded in July 2021 by Aadit Palicha (CEO) and Kaivalya Vohra (CTO) and is headquartered in Bengaluru. ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zepto_(company)))
- As of March 31, 2026, Zepto operated 1,139 dark stores. The company filed for an IPO (fresh issue of Rs 8,010 crore) allocating Rs 1,629 crore to set up 1,904 new dark stores through FY30 -- nearly doubling its current footprint -- plus Rs 1,734.9 crore for related lease payments. ([source](https://www.ciol.com/news/zeptos-udrhp-reveals-8010-cr-ipo-plan-targets-1900-new-dark-stores-12018033))
