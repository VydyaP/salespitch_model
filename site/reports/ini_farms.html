Pitch

AgroStar didn't just buy INI Farms to keep doing what it already does -- it explicitly said it's expanding into onions and vegetables, entirely new crop categories, while pushing into EU and North America markets INI Farms didn't serve before. That's more crops, more farmers, more geographies, all needing the same temperature-controlled handling that pomegranates and bananas already require -- scaling a cold chain into new categories is exactly where consistency breaks down without one system tracking every crop and every farm the same way. GND's ThinxFresh gives INI Farms that consistency from day one of the expansion, not after the first shipment goes wrong.

Opener

As you expand from pomegranates and bananas into onions and vegetables for EU and North America, how are you keeping temperature-chain consistency across crops that didn't exist in your network a year ago?
