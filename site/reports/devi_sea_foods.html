High

Five-stage cold chain across separate facility types, no stated unified tracking

Shrimp moves through a hatchery, captive farms, two processing plants, and two feed mills spread across five towns in Andhra Pradesh before export. Each handoff between stages is a point where temperature and quality data could go untracked unless one system follows the product from farm to frozen shipment -- nothing in available research names a system that does that end-to-end.

Pitch

Devi's shrimp travels through five distinct stages before it's frozen for export -- hatchery, farm, processing plant, and the feed operations feeding the farms -- spread across five different towns in Andhra Pradesh, certified to BRC, BAP, IFS, and ASC standards simultaneously. Seafood has less tolerance for a temperature slip than almost any other cold-chain product; one unified deviation-alert and compliance-reporting system across every stage, not just the final freezer, is what actually protects a shipment from farm to a US retail shelf.
