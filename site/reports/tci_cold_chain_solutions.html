Pitch

TCI has clearly invested in serious facility-level infrastructure — SCADA, VESDA, hundreds of CCTV cameras — as it's grown from 12M to 17M+ sq ft since the Mitsui JV in 2021. But that scale and pace of expansion is exactly where facility-by-facility systems start to show cracks: an auditor asking for one deviation report across the whole -18°C to +25°C network is a very different request than pulling logs from 20+ separately-built sites. GND's ThinxFresh doesn't replace what TCI already has — it sits on top, turning per-site SCADA/CCTV investment into one real-time, audit-ready view across FDA, FSSAI, NBC, and CMVR compliance at once.
