Sourced facts

  • The VH Group was founded in 1971 by Dr. B.V. Rao, who established Venkateshwara Hatcheries Pvt. Ltd. in Pune; the group is now the largest fully integrated poultry group in Asia.
    “The VH group was established in 1971, when motivated by his wife Late Smt. Uttaradevi Rao, our founder Chairman Late Padmashree Dr. B.V.Rao...established Venkateshwara Hatcheries Pvt. Ltd. in Pune (India)... Today the group is the largest fully integrated poultry group in Asia.”
  • Venky's (India) Limited operates through three segments -- Poultry and Poultry Products (day-old chicks, SPF eggs, processed chicken, poultry feed), Animal Health Products, and Oilseed.
    “The company operates through three segments: Poultry and Poultry Products, Animal Health Products, and Oilseed. The Poultry and Poultry Products segment produces and sells day old broiler and layer chicks, specific pathogen free eggs, processed chicken products, poultry feeds, and other miscellaneous poultry products.”
  • In FY25, Venky's reported total operating income of Rs 3,308.19 crore (down from Rs 3,738.66 crore in FY24), with PBILDT improving to Rs 173.05 crore (FY24: Rs 124.89 crore) at a 5.23% margin.
    “In FY25, total operating income (TOI) stood at Rs 3,308.19 crore (compared to Rs 3,738.66 crore in FY24)... Profit marked by PBILDT improved despite moderation in scale and remained at Rs 173.05 crore (FY24: Rs 124.89 crore) with a PBILDT margin of 5.23%.”
  • Venkateshwara Hatcheries' poultry operations are based across Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and the southern states of India, and the company sells processed chicken through its own retail brand, Venky's Xprs.
    “Venkateshwara Hatcheries Private Limited has based its poultry operations in Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh and the southern states of India.”

Pitch

Venky's is Asia's largest fully integrated poultry group, moving processed chicken from production facilities across Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and southern India into its own Xprs retail network -- but nothing in its public materials names a monitoring system tracking that journey. For a fresh/frozen protein business operating at this scale and multi-state spread, GND's ThinxFresh would give one consistent, auditable view from processing plant to retail counter instead of assuming everything held cold in between.
