Pitch

Dr Lal PathLabs runs one of India's largest biological sample networks -- 13,930+ pick-up points feeding 310+ labs across 28+ countries -- moving blood, tissue, and other specimens that degrade with temperature exposure. Its own site names barcode-based chain-of-custody tracking and generic 'quality checks,' but nothing about real-time temperature monitoring during transit. A barcode scan confirms a sample arrived -- it doesn't confirm it arrived at the right temperature. GND's ThinxFresh adds exactly that missing layer: instant deviation alerts and auto-generated compliance records across every pick-up point, not just proof the sample eventually showed up.
