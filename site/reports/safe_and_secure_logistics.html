Pitch

Safe & Secure already runs pharma-grade cold chain at a scale most competitors can't touch -- 475 reefers, 70+ warehouses, WHO-GDP compliance, and a genuinely strong 0.3% excursion rate across 30,000+ daily shipments. That excursion rate is checked every 15 minutes, though, not instantly -- at that volume, moving from interval logging to real-time deviation alerts is the difference between catching a problem the moment it starts and catching it up to 15 minutes late, across roughly 90 shipments a day that currently register as excursions. GND's ThinxFresh doesn't replace SSL's infrastructure -- it closes that 15-minute gap.
