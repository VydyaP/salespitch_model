Pitch

Cool Chain Logistics already engineers serious passive cold-chain packaging -- reusable systems that autonomously hold temperature for a minimum of 120 hours, with '100% monitoring of interior temperature' as a stated claim. But that's the packaging performing on its own; it's not the same as an instant alert reaching someone the moment a shipment drifts, across four countries and three regulatory regimes. GND's ThinxFresh adds the active layer on top of Cool Chain's already-strong passive engineering -- turning 'the box can survive 120 hours cold' into 'someone knows within seconds if it doesn't.'

Opener

Your reusable packaging holds temperature autonomously for 120 hours -- if a shipment does drift outside that window across Spain, the UK, the US, or Germany, who finds out first, and how fast?
