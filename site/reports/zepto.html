Dharavi warehouse in Mumbai and found fungal growth, improper storage, and inadequate cold storage -- dairy products not held at the mandated 0-4°C and frozen items failing the -18°C requirement -- plus expired products stored alongside fresh stock, resulting in license suspension under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.

Pitch

This isn't a hypothetical gap -- Maharashtra's FDA already caught Zepto's Dharavi warehouse with dairy above 4C and frozen goods above -18C, on top of expired stock mixed with fresh, and suspended its license for it. The state then ordered inspections across every quick-commerce player in Maharashtra. Zepto is now trying to fund nearly 1,900 new dark stores through its IPO by FY30 -- doubling its footprint under exactly the kind of regulatory attention that makes a repeat violation a much bigger story the second time. GND's ThinxFresh gives Zepto the thing its own regulator just proved it doesn't reliably have today: instant deviation alerts and auto-generated compliance records, provable per dark store, before an inspector has to find the problem for them.
