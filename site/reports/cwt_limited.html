Sourced facts

  • CWT was founded in 1970 as a private arm of the Port of Singapore Authority; in 2017 HNA Group completed a roughly S$1.4 billion (US$1 billion) acquisition and became controlling shareholder, renaming the company CWT International Limited.
    “CWT was founded in 1970 and was incorporated as a private arm of the Port of Singapore Authority... HNA Group planned to acquire CWT for S$1.4 billion ($1 billion)... HNA Holding Group controlled more than 90 percent of shares in CWT by October 2017... In December 2017, the company changed its name to CWT International Limited.”
  • CWT operates a 750,000-sq. ft. multi-temperature-controlled cold hub in Singapore with -18°C freezer, air-conditioned, and ambient storage, serving food manufacturers, wholesalers, and retailers.
    “750,000-sq. ft. multi-temperature-controlled cold hub facility... -18C freezer, air-conditioned and ambient storage facilities... food manufacturers, wholesalers and retailers.”
  • The facility uses a front-end iSCM platform linked to customer ERP systems, a WMS for cargo movements, and a temperature monitoring and tracking system, though no specifics on deviation-alert speed or automated compliance reporting are given.
    “Front-end iSCM platform directly links to customer ERP. WMS manages all cargo movements and handling services. Temperature monitoring and tracking system.”
  • The facility holds six separate certifications -- ISO9001, ISO14001, ISO22000, ISO22339, ISO45000, and HACCP -- and maintains separate storage for Halal and non-Halal products alongside an on-site AVA (food safety authority) Inspection Room.
    “ISO9001, ISO14001, ISO22000, ISO22339, ISO45000, HACCP certified processes... The facility maintains separate storage for Halal and non-Halal products, features an AVA Inspection Room, and provides 24/7 security.”
