High

Rapid multi-site rollout with no stated unified monitoring platform

Snowman is expanding or opening at least five facilities in 2026 alone (Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Krishnapatnam, Patna) under an asset-light Built-to-Suit model. Fast, geographically dispersed rollout like this raises the risk of siloed, per-site temperature monitoring and inconsistent compliance reporting unless every new warehouse is tied into one platform from day one.

High

Pharma cold-chain compliance load without confirmed automated audit reporting

Snowman explicitly serves pharmaceutical clients across facilities running -25°C to +20°C. India's Revised Schedule M and WHO-GDP/ALCOA+ rules require continuous deviation monitoring and audit-ready records at that scale; without a named automated compliance layer, per-site manual logging is a real regulatory exposure as pharma volume grows.

Medium

600+ vehicle reefer fleet without a stated real-time asset-tracking layer

A 600+ truck fleet moving perishable and pharma cargo pan-India is a large, distributed, high-value asset base. In-transit cold-chain breaches are typically harder to catch than in-warehouse ones, and no GPS/telematics or tamper-detection platform is named in available research.

Sourced facts

  • Snowman Logistics was incorporated in 1993 and is headquartered in Bengaluru, operating as the leading temperature-controlled logistics provider on a pan-India basis.
    “Snowman Logistics Ltd. was incorporated in 1993 and is headquartered in Bengaluru, specializing in providing end-to-end supply chain solutions, including cold storage, distribution, and value-added services. The company is the leading temperature-controlled logistics service provider operating on a pan-India basis.”
  • The company operates 50 warehouses (cold storage and dry) across 3 million+ sq ft, with a total capacity of approximately 143,000+ pallets across 40+ strategic locations as of Q3 FY2025.
    “The company boasts 50 warehouses, including cutting-edge cold storage & dry warehouses, spread across a 3 million+ square feet land area. As of Q3 FY2025, the company manages a total capacity of approximately 1,43,000+ pallets across 40+ strategic locations.”
  • Snowman operates a fleet of 600+ trucks, including primary and secondary refrigerated (reefer) vehicles, to keep the cold chain unbroken during transport.
    “The company operates a dynamic fleet of 600+ trucks. Snowman operates a large fleet of primary and secondary refrigerated trucks (reefer vehicles), ensuring that perishable goods are transported without breaking the cold chain.”
  • Snowman serves pharmaceuticals, quick-service restaurants (QSR), seafood, and e-commerce clients, and offers value-added services like blast freezing, kitting, labeling, sorting, and repacking.
    “Snowman Logistics serves industries requiring stringent temperature management, such as pharmaceuticals, quick-service restaurants (QSR), seafood, and e-commerce. The company provides specialized value-added services such as blast freezing, kitting, labeling, sorting, and repacking.”
  • In July 2026, Snowman announced a 10,000-pallet expansion of its Hyderabad facility (to 16,800 pallets total) under an asset-light Built-to-Suit model, with temperature control from -25°C to +20°C.
    “Snowman Logistics is adding 10,000 pallets of temperature-controlled storage in Hyderabad, with the expansion announced on July 15, 2026. This expands its local footprint to 16,800 pallets. The facility is being established under the company's asset-light Built-to-Suit (BTS) model... offering temperature-controlled storage capabilities ranging from -25°C to +20°C.”
  • Beyond Hyderabad, Snowman is expanding in Pune (adding a 5,900-pallet facility by June 2026 on top of an existing 16,000-pallet site) and has projects underway in Kolkata, Krishnapatnam, and Patna.
    “Beyond Hyderabad, Snowman already operates 16,000 pallets in Pune and is adding a new facility of 5,900 pallets, scheduled to be operational by June 2026. Further projects in Kolkata and Krishnapatnam are reaching operational status, and new land parcels in Patna are being identified for further reach.”
  • Gateway Distriparks is Snowman Logistics' parent/promoter company. Adani Logistics (a subsidiary of Adani Ports & SEZ) attempted to acquire a 40.25% stake in 2019 for INR 296 crore; after a 2020 dispute settlement it retained a minority ~26% stake while Gateway Distriparks remained sole promoter and manager.
    “Adani Logistics Limited (ALL), a wholly owned subsidiary of Adani Ports and SEZ Limited (APSEZ), signed an agreement to acquire 40.25% stake in Snowman Logistics Ltd. for INR 296 Crores from Gateway Distriparks Ltd... Adani Logistics continued as a minority shareholder with 26 per cent stake in Snowman Logistics following a dispute resolution between the two companies in July 2020. Gateway Distriparks continued to remain the sole promoter of Snowman and is solely responsible for the management and business operations of Snowman.”

Pitch

Snowman is scaling fast — five-plus new or expanded pan-India sites in 2026 alone — while its core business is pharma and perishables, where a single missed temperature deviation is a compliance incident, not just a customer complaint. GND's ThinxFresh platform (live BLE/LoRa sensors, instant deviation alerts, auto-generated compliance reports) gives a fast-growing multi-site network the one thing public materials don't show it already has: a single dashboard covering every warehouse and truck as it comes online.
